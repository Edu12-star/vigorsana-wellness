Five men's wellness habits that come first

Staying active after 40 is mostly about steady, ordinary routines. Healthy aging for men starts here.

Sleep

A consistent schedule and a calm wind-down routine.

Nutrition

Regular meals built around whole foods and enough protein.

Movement

Daily walking, plus strength work during the week.

Hydration

Water through the day, not only with your coffee.

Recovery

Rest days and real time to switch off from stress.

Supplements don't replace any of these habits. Some men consider them as an extra on top of a solid routine, and that is the only context for this page.
